# 🌍 Jobs and Interns – Community-Driven Job Sharing Platform

Welcome to **Jobs and Interns**, an open community platform where **anyone can share job and internship opportunities** freely with others.  
Our goal is to build a transparent, collaborative space that helps students, professionals, and job seekers connect with real opportunities — all **without any cost or restrictions**.

🌐 **Official Website:** [jobs-and-interns.github.io](https://jobs-and-interns.github.io/)

## 🚀 About the Project

**Jobs and Interns** is a **community-driven open project** designed to make job sharing simpler, faster, and more accessible.  
We believe in **knowledge sharing, not gatekeeping** — so anyone can contribute job postings or internship opportunities to help others.

- 🧑‍💼 Open for everyone — students, professionals, HRs, or companies.
- 💸 100% Free — no payments or subscriptions required.
- 🌱 Community-led — maintained and supported by volunteers.
- 💬 Instant updates — via our official WhatsApp Channel.

---

## 💡 How It Works

1. **Share Opportunities**  
   Anyone can share job vacancies, internships, or training opportunities with the community.

2. **Stay Updated**  
   Follow our WhatsApp channel for the latest updates, job postings, and internship listings.

3. **Community Driven**  
   All content is shared openly and verified by the community for better reliability.
